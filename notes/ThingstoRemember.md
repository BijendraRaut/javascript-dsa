# For every problem, before declaring it done, ask:

1. What happens with empty input?
2. What happens with one element/character?
3. What happens with duplicate values?
4. What happens with spaces?
5. What happens with null/undefined?
6. What happens with negative numbers?
7. What happens at the boundaries?
